# Canonical mule delivery lab

## Scope and method

`speedrun/lab/mule-delivery.ts` runs the real `helpers/mule.ts` role with options generated by `muleFromLedger` and `muleRunOptions`. It does not implement a second giver. Fixtures use fresh private-lab accounts, the normal tutorial skip, an empty inventory/equipment, initial positioning, and give-plus-deposit to stock the actual bank. The tutorial's 25 bank coins are retained. The default mule1 fixture grants no stat increases, quest unlocks, teleport capabilities, or delivery-time cheats. Later western measurements explicitly reproduce the separately observed h55 profile before measurement, as recorded below; they do not grant quest unlocks or a teleport kit.

The measured mule has 10 Hitpoints, every other skill at level 1, and no equipment. H4's recipient starts with 20 lab-seeded rings of recoil. Trades use the generated exact package and the recipient's real `swap`. Preparation is separate from the serving cue; readiness binds the returned preparation sequence. Each cue is scoped to the row and deterministic delivery session, and is stopped non-finally before the next row's preparation.

All timing is on **bgsrv-mac (Darwin arm64), 400 ms ticks**, with frozen root/webclient/gateway/engine installs and engine build (`BUILD_VERIFY=false BUILD_LIVE_RELOAD=false`) in the same batch. Independent ports: 9540, 9541, 9542, 45440, 8540. `lab.sh` bounds execution and cleans up its own children. No public-world login or fleet edits.

A mechanically successful harness run is **not** scored arrival acceptance. The harness separately enforces a **132-tick comparison** for H4→H4k (nonzero exit when exceeded); 206 is reported only as an arrival comparison. The recipient intentionally waits for readiness before walking to the second trade, so its eventual trade cannot establish that a live scored runner would arrive on time.

**Latest carry-ahead result:** both real trades complete, all 20 returned rings remain with the mule through H4k and are deposited afterward, and fresh H4k readiness arrives **44 ticks** after H4 success, at revision `dc27ba3b`. That is 88 ticks below the conservative 132-tick model, not continuous scored-chain acceptance. The preserved bank-return baseline took **132 ticks**, with zero model margin. H1 also completes mechanically, with 97 ticks of preparation and varying observed damage.
